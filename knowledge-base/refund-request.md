# How do I ask for a refund?

You can ask for a refund within **30 days** after your order arrives.

## Before you start

- The item should be unused, in its box.
- You need your order number.

## Steps

1. Log in and click **My orders**.
2. Click the order, then **Return item**.
3. Choose the item and the reason.
4. Print the free return label, or save the QR code.
5. Pack the item and give it to the courier.
6. You will get an email when we receive it.

## When will I get my money?

- We check the item within **3 business days** after we receive it.
- The refund goes back to the same card or payment method.
- Your bank may need **5–10 business days** to show it.

## Things that are not refundable

- Gift cards
- Items that were used or damaged by the customer
- Items returned after 30 days

If your case is different, write to us. We will always look at it.
