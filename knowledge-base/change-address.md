# How do I change my delivery address?

You can change the address **before the order ships**.

## Steps

1. Log in and click **My orders**.
2. Open the order. Check the status.
3. If it says **Processing**, click **Edit address**, type the new one, and click **Save**.
4. You will get a new confirmation email.

## If the order already shipped

We cannot change the address inside our system. But you can:

1. Open the **Track package** page and look for a "Change delivery" option from the courier.
2. Or write to us right away. We will ask the courier to help. We cannot promise it will work, but we always try.

## Tip

Check the address at checkout, including the apartment number and phone number. The courier needs both.
