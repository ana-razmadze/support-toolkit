# My payment did not go through

Don't worry. This happens often and is usually easy to fix.

## Try these first

1. Check the card number, date and 3-digit code. One wrong number is the most common reason.
2. Check that the billing address matches the one your bank has.
3. Check that you have enough money on the card.
4. Try another card or another payment method.
5. Try a different browser, or turn off VPN or ad blockers.

## Money left my account but I have no order

Sometimes the bank keeps the money for a short time. This is a **hold**, not a payment.

- The hold disappears by itself in **3–5 business days**.
- If you see a confirmation email, your order is fine.
- If you see no email after 1 hour, write to us with the last 4 digits of your card and the time you paid. Never send the full card number.
