# How do I reset my password?

Forgot your password? No problem. It takes about 2 minutes.

## Steps

1. Go to the BrightCart login page.
2. Click **Forgot password?** under the password box.
3. Type the email you used to sign up. Click **Send link**.
4. Open your email. Look for a message called "Reset your password".
5. Click the button in the email. The link works for **30 minutes**.
6. Type a new password. Use at least 10 characters, with one number.
7. Click **Save**. You will see "Password changed".

## I did not get the email

- Check your **Spam** or **Junk** folder.
- Check that you typed your email correctly (for example, no extra space).
- Wait 5 minutes and try again.
- Try another email you may have used.

## Tips

- Do not share your password with anyone. BrightCart will never ask for it.
- Use a different password for each website.
