# Where is my order?

You can check your order in 1 minute.

## Steps

1. Log in to your BrightCart account.
2. Click **My orders**.
3. Click your order number.
4. Look at the **Status**.

## What each status means

| Status | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Processing | We are packing your order | Nothing. It usually takes 1–2 days. |
| Shipped | Your order left our warehouse | Click **Track package** to see where it is. |
| Out for delivery | The courier is bringing it today | Be ready to receive it. |
| Delivered | The courier says it arrived | Check your door, neighbors and mailbox. |
| Delayed | There is a problem on the way (weather, courier) | Wait 2 more days. We will email you. |

## My order is late

Normal delivery is 3–7 business days. If it is later than 7 business days:

1. Check the tracking page first.
2. If tracking has not changed for 3 days, write to us.
3. Send your order number. We will contact the courier and tell you what we find within 24 hours.

## It says "Delivered" but I do not have it

1. Check around your home and with neighbors.
2. Wait 24 hours. Sometimes the status is updated early.
3. If it is still missing, write to us. We will open a search with the courier, and send a new item or refund you if the package is lost.
