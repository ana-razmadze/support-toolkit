# I received the wrong or damaged item

We are sorry about that. We will fix it.

## Steps

1. Take **2 photos**: one of the item, one of the box and the shipping label.
2. Write to support@brightcart.example within 14 days.
3. Include your order number and the photos.
4. Tell us what you prefer: a **new item** or a **refund**.

## What happens next

- We reply within 24 hours.
- You do **not** need to send the wrong item back if it is cheap. For expensive items we send a free return label.
- A new item ships in 1–2 business days. A refund takes 5–10 business days to show in your bank.

## Tip

Keep the box until your case is closed. It helps us work with the courier.
