# Reply Templates

Ready replies for common hard moments. Each one follows the same shape:
**1) Show I understand → 2) Say what I will do → 3) Say when → 4) Thank them.**

Every template is short on purpose. Real customers read fast and are often upset.

---

## 1. Order is late

Hi Maria,

I am sorry your order is late. I know that is frustrating, especially when you were waiting for it.

I checked with the courier. Your package is delayed because of bad weather, and the new delivery date is Thursday. I will check again on Wednesday morning and write to you if anything changes.

If it does not arrive by Thursday, reply to this email and I will send a new one or refund you, your choice.

Thank you for your patience.

Ana

---

## 2. Customer is very angry

Hi James,

You are right to be upset, and I am sorry this happened. You paid for a service and did not get what you expected.

Here is what I am doing right now: I am checking your case myself and I will send you a clear answer by 5 pm today. You do not need to explain it again, I have all the details from your earlier messages.

Thank you for telling us. It helps us fix this.

Ana

---

## 3. We cannot give a refund

Hi Sofia,

Thank you for writing to us. I understand you want your money back, and I looked at your case carefully.

Our policy allows refunds for 30 days. Your order arrived 45 days ago, so I cannot refund it. I am sorry, I know this is not the answer you hoped for.

What I **can** do: give you a 20% discount for your next order, or check if the item has a repair option. Tell me which you prefer and I will do it today.

Ana

---

## 4. We need more information

Hi Daniel,

Thank you for contacting us. I want to solve this fast, so I need two things from you:

1. Your order number (it starts with BC-)
2. A photo of the problem

When I have these, I will reply within 24 hours with the next step.

Ana

---

## 5. Problem is fixed (follow-up)

Hi Elena,

Good news: your problem is fixed. Your refund was sent today and should appear in your bank in 5–10 business days.

Please try it again and tell me if anything is still wrong. I will keep your ticket open for 3 days, just in case.

Have a great day,
Ana

---

## 6. Passing the ticket to another team

Hi Peter,

I want to make sure you get the right answer, so I am passing your case to our payments team. They have the tools to check this in detail.

You do not need to repeat anything. I gave them the full story. They will contact you within 24 hours. If you do not hear from them, reply here and I will follow up personally.

Ana

---

## 7. Saying sorry for our mistake

Hi Nina,

This was our mistake, and I am sorry. We sent you the wrong size.

I already sent the correct size with free express shipping. It will arrive in 2–3 days. Please keep the wrong one, there is no need to return it.

Thank you for giving us a chance to fix it.

Ana

---

## Notes to myself

- Use the customer's name.
- Never write "unfortunately" three times in one email.
- Always give a day or a time, never "soon".
- If I do not know the answer, I say so and say when I will find out.
