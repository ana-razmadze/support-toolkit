# Support Toolkit

Hi, I'm Ana. I work in customer support and operations. I have 3+ years of experience with B2B and B2C clients, calls, emails, chats and social media.

This repo is my support portfolio. It shows how I write, how I solve problems, and how I think about process.

**All company names, customers and numbers in this repo are made up.** The company is called "BrightCart", a pretend online shop. No real customer data is used.

## How I work

1. **Listen first.** Understand the real problem before I answer.
2. **Be clear and calm.** Short sentences. No confusing words.
3. **Fix the cause, not only the ticket.** If the same question comes 10 times, write an article.
4. **Follow up.** Tell the customer what happens next and when.

Zendesk, Intercom, Help Scout, Gorgias, HubSpot, Bitrix24, Shopify, Slack, Jira, Notion, Google Workspace. I also use AI tools (Claude, NotebookLM) to work faster, and I always check the answers before I use them.
